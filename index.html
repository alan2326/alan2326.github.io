About

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at UT Austin, advised by Swarat Chaudhuri. I am broadly interested in the application of AI to mathematics.

One project I am currently excited about uses AI to improve the best known bounds on the Grothendieck constant, which is fundamental to areas such as functional analysis, combinatorial optimization, and theoretical computer science (see our recent preprint). In this project, we have been exploring how AI can collaborate with human mathematicians on long-horizon open research problems.

Please feel free to reach out if you'd like to talk about any of this!
